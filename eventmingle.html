Event Management Website UX Concept Case Study #04

EventMingle: Reimagining Event Management Websites & Apps

EventMingle is an event management web experience designed for discovering events and managing ticket sales with streamlined workflows.

Role UI/UX Designer
Scope Website UX/UI Concept
Methodology Google Surveys & App Reviews Analysis
Designer Pavithra S.
Strategic Foundations

Problem Statement & Product Scope

Defining core ticketing bottlenecks, platform feedback insights, and boundaries for the redesign concept.

Problem Statement

Ticketing & Platform Friction

Event organizers and attendees face severe friction on existing platforms due to slow website performance, difficult refund processing, unresponsive customer service, and rigid ticket customization options.

Design Opportunity

Unified Event Management Experience

There is an opportunity to build a reliable ecosystem that combines fast responsive web design, transparent fee structures, robust customer support, and seamless ticketing tools.

How Might We?

Simplifying Event Logistics

How might we streamline event administration and ticket purchasing while ensuring reliable accessibility, responsive support, and improved customization for a better experience?

Product Scope

EventMingle Platform & App Ecosystem

The scope focuses on resolving pain points identified in competitor app reviews and quantitative surveys, streamlining organizer dashboard interactions, payment processing, and attendee ticketing flows.

UX Research & Competitive Analysis

Research Insights & Competitor Matrix

Analyzing user feedback, survey data, and app reviews to ground EventMingle in real user needs.

Competitor Analysis Matrix

Aspect Competitors (SimpleTix, Eventify, Ticketmaster) EventMingle (Proposed)
Ticketing & Fees High processing fees and hidden service charges. Transparent pricing with low structural platform fees.
Organizer Tools Rigid customization for branding and event setup. Advanced customization studio for custom registration and ticket tiers.

App Store Reviews Analysis

Key User Feedback & Pain Points

Analysis of user feedback reveals repeated complaints regarding website sluggishness, checkout crashes, complicated refund processes, and unresponsive customer service. EventMingle addresses these directly through high-performance web architecture, simplified ticketing flows, and dedicated support tools.

System Structure

Platform Navigation & Core Flows

1. Discovery & Feed

Fast event browsing, category filters, and search.

2. Ticketing & Checkout

Frictionless seat selection and secure payment processing.

3. Organizer Dashboard

Custom event creation, graphics tools, and sales tracking.

4. Support & Vault

Active ticket wallet, responsive help center, and payouts.

Feature Concepts

Key Feature Breakdown

Targeting major pain points from competitor reviews with dedicated design solutions.

01 Discovery

Seamless Event Discovery

A fast, lightweight browsing experience allowing users to quickly find events, filter by categories, and view verified details without website lag or connection drops.

02 Checkout

Reliable Payment & Ticketing

An optimized checkout architecture that eliminates processing glitches, prevents accidental double charges, and securely stores digital tickets for instant offline access.

03 Organizer

Advanced Customization Studio

Empowers event creators to easily design custom registration forms, ticket tiers, and promotional graphics without rigid layout constraints.

04 Support & Payouts

Transparent Payouts & Support

Direct access to responsive customer support and transparent, rapid fund disbursement to resolve common organizer complaints regarding withheld revenues.

Case Study Conclusion

Elevating the Event Experience

Through EventMingle, user research and app reviews directly informed an ecosystem that tackles processing glitches, poor customer support, and rigid event setup, establishing a smoother path for both organizers and attendees.
