Food & Recipe Sharing Web UX Concept Case Study #02

FeastFinder: A Community-Driven Recipe Sharing Platform

FeastFinder is an all-in-one platform for culinary enthusiasts offering comprehensive recipe collections, an easy-to-use ingredient-based search filter, step-by-step cooking guides, and a community space for sharing recipes.

Role Sole UI/UX Designer
Scope Recipe Web Application
Methodology Surveys & User Interviews
Designer Pavithra S.
Strategic Foundations

Problem Statement & Product Scope

Defining core cooking friction points, culinary research insights, and boundaries for the website concept.

Problem Statement

Pantry Waste & Cluttered Recipes

The current food & recipe sharing webs present fragmented step-by-step instructions, poor ingredient matching, and lack effective filtering for dietary preferences, leading to meal decision paralysis and kitchen waste.

Design Opportunity

Unified Culinary Space

A clean ecosystem combining ingredient-based smart discovery, distraction-free cooking modes, verified user reviews, and effortless community recipe uploading.

How Might We?

Simplifying Home Cooking

How might we help home cooks effortlessly find meals based on available ingredients while building an engaging platform for recipe creators to share their culinary passion?

Product Scope

FeastFinder Ecosystem

The web platform scope centers on resolving ingredient discovery gaps, optimizing recipe detail views with interactive checkboxes/scaling, streamlining community submission flows, and structuring intuitive category navigation.

UX Research & Target Audience

Target Audience & Survey Data

Understanding user behaviors and cooking habits through quantitative survey insights and audience segmentations.

Demographics & Audience Segments

Subscribers / Foodies

Users seeking new recipes based on available home ingredients, healthy meal prep options, and step-by-step cooking directions.

Content Creators / Chefs

Home cooks and culinary bloggers looking to publish custom recipes, grow an audience, and receive community feedback.

Key Quantitative Metrics

68.4% Search recipes by available ingredients
52.6% Prefer video & visual step guides
45% Filter meals by dietary restrictions
84% Rely on community ratings & reviews
S

Sarah

Working Professional & Home Cook

Sarah works full-time and enjoys cooking healthy meals after work. She gets frustrated when recipe sites are cluttered with ads and long blog intro posts instead of clear instructions.

Frustrations
  • Cluttered screens filled with ads
  • Difficulty finding recipes with existing pantry items
  • Inaccurate preparation & cook time estimates
Needs & Goals
  • Quick search by ingredients
  • Step-by-step distraction-free cook view
  • Saved favorite recipe collections

Feature Concepts

Key Feature Breakdown

Addressing home cooks' biggest frustrations with intuitive, user-centered culinary solutions.

01 Smart Discovery

Pantry-Based Recipe Finder

An intelligent search tool allowing users to input ingredients currently in their fridge and pantry, surfacing instant recipe matches to reduce food waste and grocery hassle.

02 Hands-Free Cooking

Video & Step-by-Step Guides

Distraction-free cook mode featuring embedded HD video previews, interactive step checkboxes, dynamic timer widgets, and a keep-awake screen setting for seamless cooking.

03 Community Hub

Recipe Sharing & Creator Studio

A drag-and-drop recipe builder empowering foodies and creators to publish custom dishes, upload step-by-step photos, and build an active community following.

04 World Cuisines

Global Variety & Dietary Filters

Explore curated recipe collections spanning top global cuisines—from Authentic Asian to Mediterranean—with instant filters for Vegan, Gluten-Free, Keto, and prep-time needs.

UI Showcase

Figma High-Fidelity Website Mockups

Full-width desktop website frames featuring Discover Your Favorite Recipes and Explore, Read & Save Recipe Articles. Scroll within each container to view the complete layouts.

01.Discover Your Favorite Recipes

Explore the Feast Finder homepage to discover and choose your preferred recipes from a variety of categories based on your tastes and interests.

02. Explore, Read & Save Recipe Articles

Discover food and recipe articles, read your favorite ones, and easily share or save them for future reference.

03. Share Your Special Recipes

Upload your favorite or unique recipes and share them with the Feast Finder community to inspire others and exchange cooking ideas.

04. Cook Your Favorite Way

Explore the detailed view of your favorite recipe, follow the written instructions, prepare the dish yourself, or watch a video tutorial based on your preferred cooking style.

Case Study Conclusion

Crafting Joyful Digital Culinary Experiences

FeastFinder demonstrates how user-centered wireframing and empathetic visual design can turn everyday meal preparation into a delightful, social experience..
